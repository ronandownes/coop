## What is the sector?

Large healthcare and public-sector organisations have many of the same quantitative and financial problems as private organisations: budgets, staffing, capacity, procurement, forecasting, reporting, controls and performance measurement.

The difference is that financial and operational decisions are ultimately connected to public service delivery.

## Where Financial Mathematics fits

Relevant skills include numerical analysis, statistics, forecasting, optimisation, data analysis and communicating quantitative information clearly.

Possible roles include finance analyst, business analyst, planning analyst, performance analyst, operations analyst, management accounting support and data or reporting roles.

## Why this matters for an internship

A hospital or public organisation can give exposure to large real-world datasets, formal financial controls, multidisciplinary teams and decisions where resources have to be allocated carefully.

## Application lens

For these roles, foreground **accuracy, reliability, data analysis, budgeting or financial awareness, communication, teamwork and the ability to explain numbers to non-specialists**.

A specific employer study can sit under this sector whenever a live hospital or public-sector opportunity is being prepared.
