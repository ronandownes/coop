## What is the sector?

Aviation finance connects a physical aircraft with capital, contracts, operators and long-term asset value. Aircraft leasing and asset management therefore combine finance, data, legal agreements, technical condition, maintenance, risk and commercial relationships.

For a Financial Mathematics student, it is a useful example of mathematics and data being applied to a real, high-value asset.

## Where the maths and data appear

Typical analytical areas include lease cash flows, utilisation, maintenance costs and reserves, residual value, credit risk, scenario analysis, portfolio reporting and asset valuation.

The work also requires accurate asset records and communication with finance, commercial, legal, technical and airline counterparties.
