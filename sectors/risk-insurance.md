## What is the sector?

Risk and insurance are built around uncertain future outcomes. Organisations need to estimate the likelihood and financial effect of events, decide how much risk to retain, price it appropriately and hold enough capital to remain resilient.

## Where Financial Mathematics fits

Probability and statistics are central. So are modelling, simulation, time series, optimisation and careful interpretation of assumptions.

Possible roles include risk analyst, actuarial analyst, credit risk analyst, market risk analyst, insurance analyst and model validation or reporting roles.

## Interview lens

Strong answers should show that a model is not just a calculation. The assumptions, data quality, uncertainty and business decision all matter.

Projects involving forecasting, stress testing, scenario analysis or probability can become strong evidence here.
