## What is the sector?

This area covers organisations that lend, invest, manage capital, trade financial instruments or advise on financing decisions.

Possible graduate and internship roles include financial analyst, investment analyst, credit analyst, treasury analyst, markets analyst, portfolio analyst and quantitative analyst.

## Where Financial Mathematics fits

The degree connects directly to time value of money, probability, statistics, optimisation, numerical methods, risk, modelling and financial decision-making.

Technical skills such as Python, Excel, SQL and data visualisation can support pricing, portfolio analysis, credit work, scenario testing and reporting.

## What to investigate for an employer

Understand **how the organisation makes money, what risks it takes, what assets or clients it serves, what decisions the team makes and what information the intern will actually handle**.

A good employer study should then translate the job description into evidence from modules, projects and work experience.
