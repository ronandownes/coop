## What is the sector?

Data and FinTech roles use software and quantitative methods to improve financial products, reporting, operations, controls and decision-making.

The work can sit inside a technology company, bank, insurer, investment firm, aircraft lessor, CSP or almost any large organisation.

## Where Financial Mathematics fits

A Financial Mathematics student brings the numerical context needed to understand what the data represents, while programming and analytics tools help work at scale.

Useful tools include **Python, Pandas, SQL, Excel, Power BI, R and MATLAB** depending on the role.

## Typical work

Examples include data cleaning, reporting automation, dashboards, forecasting, anomaly checking, process improvement, KPI analysis, financial modelling and decision support.

## Application lens

Do not simply list software. Show **what problem was solved, what data was used, what checks were performed and what decision or insight the output supported**.
