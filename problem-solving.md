<div class="question"><h3>Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><p>Choose a problem where you had to diagnose the issue rather than simply follow instructions. Explain the information available, the options you considered, the method you chose and how you checked the outcome.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>diagnose</span><span>assumptions</span><span>method</span><span>validation</span></div><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Problem → method → check → result.</p></div>
<div class="question"><h3>Tell me about a mistake you made.</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><p>Use a real but manageable example. Own the error, explain how you corrected it, then show what process you changed so that the same mistake was less likely to happen again.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>ownership</span><span>correction</span><span>learning</span><span>process improvement</span></div></div>
<div class="question"><h3>How do you approach a problem when you do not immediately know the answer?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer</p><p>I break the problem into smaller parts, identify what is known and unknown, check whether I have seen a similar structure before, make reasonable assumptions explicit and test the result. If I am still blocked, I would ask for help with a clear explanation of what I have already tried.</p><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Break down → assumptions → test → ask intelligently.</p></div>
