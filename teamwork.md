## Successful team | Tell me about a time you worked successfully in a team.

Use STAR briefly: set the context, explain your role, describe the actions you personally took, and finish with the result and what you learned about working with others.

role claritycommunicationreliabilityshared goal

**Recall cue:** Situation → my role → my action → result.

## Disagreement | Tell me about a disagreement within a group.

Show that you can disagree without becoming defensive. Explain how you listened, clarified the issue, used evidence where possible and helped the group move towards a workable decision.

listenclarifyevidencecompromise

## Explain clearly | How would you explain something technical to a non-technical person?

I would first identify what the person actually needs to understand, remove unnecessary jargon, use a simple example or visual if useful, and check understanding rather than assuming that a clear explanation to me is automatically clear to them.

**Recall cue:** Audience → simplify → example → check.
