<div class="question"><h3>Tell me about a time you worked successfully in a team.</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><p>Use STAR briefly: set the context, explain your role, describe the actions you personally took, and finish with the result and what you learned about working with others.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>role clarity</span><span>communication</span><span>reliability</span><span>shared goal</span></div><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Situation → my role → my action → result.</p></div>
<div class="question"><h3>Tell me about a disagreement within a group.</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><p>Show that you can disagree without becoming defensive. Explain how you listened, clarified the issue, used evidence where possible and helped the group move towards a workable decision.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>listen</span><span>clarify</span><span>evidence</span><span>compromise</span></div></div>
<div class="question"><h3>How would you explain something technical to a non-technical person?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer</p><p>I would first identify what the person actually needs to understand, remove unnecessary jargon, use a simple example or visual if useful, and check understanding rather than assuming that a clear explanation to me is automatically clear to them.</p><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Audience → simplify → example → check.</p></div>
