## Why the work interests me

What appeals to me about asset management is how many things come together: the asset, the customer, the contract, the finances and the information behind each decision. I enjoy finding patterns and logic in problems that seem complicated at first. The inputs are not just numbers. Relationships, contracts, regulation and wider economic changes matter too.

I have always been fascinated by aircraft. Studying the sector has helped me see an aircraft as both an engineering asset and a financial asset.

## What I have explored

I built an interactive fleet dashboard using public data to investigate aircraft and where they operate. Working through gaps and inconsistencies in the sources taught me to check what a result really means rather than accepting the first number I find.

## What I would bring

I understand that an internship also involves careful day-to-day work. Reports must be accurate, data in internal systems must be reliable, and repeated processes need consistent attention.

I use Excel and other data tools, and I would like to develop those skills further in a professional reporting environment. I am particularly interested in work that combines data, finance, accuracy, communication and process improvement.
