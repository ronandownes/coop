## Asset management | What does asset management mean in aircraft leasing?

The aircraft is both a **physical asset and a financial asset**. Asset management means keeping reliable information about it, monitoring contractual and technical obligations, understanding its condition and use, and helping protect its value throughout the lease.

**Recall cue:** aircraft → lease → condition → obligations → value.

### Lease Agreement and technical condition

The Lease Agreement defines contractual obligations and reporting dates. Maintenance condition affects availability, future cost, transition planning and ultimately asset value.

agreement | deadline | maintenance | redelivery | value

## Regional aviation | Why do turboprops matter?

On suitable shorter sectors, turboprops can match capacity and operating economics to regional demand while serving airports that may have shorter runways or lower passenger volumes.

route | capacity | trip economics | regional access
