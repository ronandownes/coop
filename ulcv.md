> **Use the Pivotal Corporate version for the Pivotal Corporate application. For the HSE Finance application, switch to the HSE Finance version below.**  
> The General ULCV section is the master evidence bank: keep the underlying examples true, then change the emphasis and vocabulary for the role.

## General ULCV

### General — Teamwork

Use the strongest evidence of working reliably with other people: university group work, employment, shared tasks, handovers and supporting colleagues.

### General — Communication Skills

Show clear written and verbal communication, including explaining technical material simply, writing and rewriting carefully, and using digital tools appropriately while keeping human judgement in the loop.

### General — Problem Solving and Analytics

Draw on Financial Mathematics, statistics, data analysis and practical problem solving. Use specific examples where a problem was investigated, checked and communicated rather than simply giving a final answer.

### General — Using Initiative

Use examples where work was moved forward without waiting to be told every step: identifying what needed to be done, learning a tool, improving a process or resolving a practical issue.

### General — Projects, Portfolio and Volunteering

Use the strongest relevant projects from the website and portfolio. Prioritise projects that demonstrate analysis, organisation, communication, independent learning and a finished output.

### General — Additional Information

Keep supporting information here: software, technical tools, interests, work experience and any other evidence that strengthens the application without fitting naturally under the five headings above.

## Pivotal Corporate ULCV

### Pivotal Corporate — Teamwork

Emphasise professional working relationships, client-service teamwork, internal clients, external clients and intermediaries. Use retail, logistics and university examples that show reliability, cooperation and good handovers.

### Pivotal Corporate — Communication Skills

Emphasise clear, professional written and verbal communication. Connect this to client service, accurate emails and documents, explaining information clearly, and careful final review of AI-assisted or technical work.

### Pivotal Corporate — Problem Solving and Analytics

Emphasise accuracy, reconciliation, financial information, Excel, data checking and practical analysis. Connect quantitative study to the day-to-day discipline of corporate administration and accounting support.

### Pivotal Corporate — Using Initiative

Emphasise being proactive: keeping work moving, following up, spotting what needs attention, improving a process and taking ownership while knowing when to ask for guidance.

### Pivotal Corporate — Projects, Portfolio and Volunteering

Prioritise projects showing finance, data handling, spreadsheets, reporting, process improvement and professional presentation. The purpose is to show evidence of useful work, not simply a list of technologies.

### Pivotal Corporate — Additional Information

Prioritise commercial awareness, client focus, organisation, Microsoft Office / Excel, interest in finance and the ability to support accounting and administration work accurately.

## HSE Finance ULCV

### HSE Finance — Teamwork

Emphasise working constructively with colleagues across a large organisation, supporting shared deadlines, respecting different roles and contributing reliably to a service-focused team.

### HSE Finance — Communication Skills

Emphasise clear communication of financial and numerical information to both finance and non-finance colleagues. Use examples of listening carefully, writing clearly and explaining analysis without unnecessary jargon.

### HSE Finance — Problem Solving and Analytics

Emphasise numerical accuracy, financial analysis, spreadsheets, data quality, checking, reporting and the ability to investigate discrepancies methodically.

### HSE Finance — Using Initiative

Emphasise taking responsibility for assigned work, following issues through, learning systems quickly, improving routine processes and escalating appropriately when something needs attention.

### HSE Finance — Projects, Portfolio and Volunteering

Prioritise projects that show structured analysis, data handling, reporting, public-service relevance, financial understanding and the ability to turn information into a useful output.

### HSE Finance — Additional Information

Prioritise Financial Mathematics, accounting and finance modules, Excel and data skills, accuracy, confidentiality, organisation and interest in contributing to a public-service finance environment.
