Strong results include A1 in Introduction to Probability and Statistics and A2 grades in Finance, Applied Data Analysis, Numerical Analysis, Operations Research, Calculus and several other quantitative modules.

## How to use this site

Open one section at a time. Read the question, rehearse the settled answer aloud, then close it and try again from the recall cue and key vocabulary rather than memorising every sentence word-for-word.
