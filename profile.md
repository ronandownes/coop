His studies combine finance, probability and statistics, numerical analysis, operations research and software development. He has also built a growing portfolio of projects, including financial calculators, interactive data visualisations and mapping applications. These projects have given him experience taking an idea from raw data through analysis and development to something another person can use and understand.
    </p>
