## Why Aviation?

I’ve always had a fascination with flight. Every time I fly, I still find it incredible that engineering, mathematics, science and technology all come together to put an aircraft in the air safely and reliably.

Studying aviation finance has helped me see an aircraft as both an engineering asset and a financial asset, which connects naturally with Financial Mathematics and Data Analysis.

What interests me about asset management is how many things come together: lease rates, maintenance costs, utilisation, residual values and cash flows, but also aircraft type, condition, operator, location and the lease agreement itself.

There is also a strong relationship side. Aircraft leasing depends on long-term trust between lessors, airlines, technical teams, financiers and advisers.

That combination of aviation, data, finance and relationships is what interests me.
