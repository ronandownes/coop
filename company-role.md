<div class="question"><h3>Why did you apply to our company?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><ul><li>One specific fact about the company or team.</li><li>Why that work interests you.</li><li>How your Financial Mathematics background connects.</li><li>What you hope to learn and contribute.</li></ul><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Them → me → fit → contribution.</p></div>
<div class="question"><h3>What interests you about this role?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><p>Focus on the actual work rather than saying only that it is a good opportunity. Mention analysis, data, modelling, systems, finance, operations or client work where relevant, then connect that to modules and projects you have already completed.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>role requirements</span><span>transferable skills</span><span>learning</span><span>contribution</span></div></div>
<div class="question"><h3>Why should we choose you?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer structure</p><p>Give three grounded reasons: strong quantitative preparation, evidence that you can learn unfamiliar material, and a professional attitude to working with others. Keep it factual and avoid overselling.</p><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Quantitative ability → learning ability → dependable colleague.</p></div>
