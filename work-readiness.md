<div class="question"><h3>How would you deal with a task you had never done before?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer</p><p>I would clarify the expected outcome, check available instructions or examples, break the task into steps, make a first attempt, and ask focused questions where needed. I would rather ask a precise question early than continue with a wrong assumption.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>clarify</span><span>independent first attempt</span><span>focused questions</span><span>feedback</span></div></div>

<div class="question"><h3>What would you do if you realised you had made an error?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer</p><p>I would identify the impact, correct it as quickly as possible, tell the appropriate person if the error affects others, and review why it happened so that I can improve the process rather than just patch the immediate problem.</p><div class="key-vocab"><span>ownership</span><span>impact</span><span>correction</span><span>prevention</span></div></div>

<div class="question"><h3>What does professionalism mean to you?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer</p><p>Being reliable, communicating clearly, respecting confidentiality, meeting commitments, taking feedback seriously and behaving in a way that makes it easy for colleagues to trust your work.</p><p class="recall"><strong>Recall cue:</strong> Reliable → clear → confidential → accountable.</p></div>

<div class="question"><h3>What would you do during a quiet period if you had finished your work?</h3><p class="answer-label">Answer</p><p>I would check that my current work is complete, ask whether there is anything else that needs attention, and use the time constructively to learn systems, review documentation or understand the wider work of the team rather than waiting passively.</p></div>
