## Global markets, localisation & AI

A strong international product experience should not feel like English content that has simply been translated.

AI can work **behind the scenes** to help adapt product information for customers, airlines, lessors or partners in different countries while keeping the facts, commercial choices and brand voice controlled.

A useful architecture would separate:

**Source truth → market context → language adaptation → human review → published experience**

Possible uses include:

- explaining aircraft and leasing choices in the user's own language
- adapting examples, units, currencies and terminology to the local market
- answering product questions conversationally
- translating technical material while preserving approved terminology
- generating country-specific landing pages from one controlled source of truth
- showing locally relevant aircraft, routes or operating considerations where verified data exist
- learning which questions different markets ask most often

The important principle is **authentic localisation, not uncontrolled generation**. Product facts, prices, fleet data and legal/commercial claims should come from approved sources; AI handles expression, discovery and adaptation around that verified core.

## Decision-support dashboards

The strongest dashboards are communication tools as well as analytical tools.

They should help a user move from:

**data → pattern → comparison → implication → decision**

That is where mathematics, statistics, software and commercial judgement meet.
