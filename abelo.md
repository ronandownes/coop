Abelo: Reaching new heights in the turboprop sector



**Abelo is a  B2B aircraft lessor, not a consumer-facing airline**. It was only created in **2022**, from the combination of Elix Aviation and ADARE Aviation Capital, so it doesn’t have the long public/media footprint of companies like AerCap or Avolon.



### Erik's 30-second summary

**Abelo is a Dublin-based B2B aircraft lessor specialising in regional turboprop aircraft.** It does not sell tickets to passengers. It owns or finances aircraft and places them with airlines, then manages the commercial, financial and technical life of those assets.

The business sits at the intersection of **finance, aircraft, data, asset management, risk and sustainability**. For Erik, that is the important connection: a Financial Mathematics degree can be applied to real assets with long lives, large capital values and uncertain future cash flows.

### What has changed since Abelo was founded?

Abelo was created in **2022** and has moved quickly from a relatively new platform into a growing specialist lessor.

- **May 2025 — Cerberus acquired Abelo** from funds managed by Oaktree Capital Management.
- **2025 — Abelo secured a warehouse financing facility of up to $750 million** to support fleet and customer growth.
- **March 2026 — Abelo said it had 36 firm ATR aircraft ordered**, with another nine options and purchase rights.
- Its newer placements show an increasingly international customer base across **Europe, Latin America, Africa, Asia and Australia**.

That growth matters because an aircraft lessor is not simply buying planes. It has to decide **which aircraft to buy, how to finance them, which airlines and markets to place them with, what lease structure to use, how to manage technical transitions, and what the aircraft may be worth years later**.

### The aircraft strategy | ATR 42 and ATR 72

Abelo's strategy is centred on larger regional turboprops, particularly the **ATR 42-600** and **ATR 72-600**.

The ATR 42 serves the smaller end of the regional market, while the ATR 72 offers greater capacity. The attraction is not simply that they have propellers: modern turboprops are designed for shorter regional sectors where a jet may be unnecessarily expensive or inefficient.

Abelo and ATR have also adjusted the orderbook in response to market demand. In January 2025, an earlier order for ten ATR 42 STOL aircraft was converted into **five ATR 42-600 and five ATR 72-600 aircraft**, with three further ATR 72-600s added.

### What one transaction actually involves

A useful example is the February 2026 transition of an **ATR 72-500 to Air Navigator Group / Aerlink in Australia**. Abelo said the work included **repossession from Blue Islands, inspection, maintenance and reconfiguration to the new operator's specification in less than 100 days**.

That is a good picture of aircraft asset management. The job does not stop when a lease is signed. A lessor has to coordinate technical condition, documentation, maintenance, transition timing, customer requirements and the economics of keeping an expensive asset earning revenue.

### How Abelo makes money | Think like an asset manager

At a simplified level, the commercial model is:

**Raise capital → acquire aircraft → lease aircraft → collect lease cash flows → manage risk and maintenance → transition or sell the aircraft → manage residual value.**

For Erik, the Financial Mathematics links are immediate:

- **Cash-flow modelling:** lease rentals, deposits, maintenance reserves and financing payments.
- **Present value:** comparing future lease income with the price paid for the asset.
- **Interest-rate risk:** aircraft are capital-intensive and financing costs matter.
- **Credit risk:** the airline must remain capable of meeting its lease obligations.
- **Residual-value risk:** what will the aircraft be worth at the end of a lease?
- **Portfolio risk:** diversification by airline, region, aircraft type and lease maturity.
- **Scenario analysis:** fuel prices, rates, inflation, airline demand and aircraft values can all change.
- **Data quality:** commercial decisions are only as good as the underlying fleet, lease and market data.

### Growth and financing | Why the $750m facility matters

A warehouse facility gives a lessor a pool of financing that can be drawn to acquire aircraft before those assets are refinanced, sold or moved into longer-term structures.

For interview purposes, Erik does not need to become an aviation-finance lawyer. The important point is that **fleet growth requires both attractive aircraft opportunities and access to capital**. Abelo's 2025 facility was underwritten by Deutsche Bank, with Deutsche Bank and MUFG as joint lead arrangers.

Abelo had also previously announced a **$190 million financing facility for a 20-turboprop portfolio**, involving MUFG, Deutsche Bank and Société Générale. That gives Erik a concrete example of how aviation leasing brings together aircraft, banks, investors and asset-management decisions.

### Sustainability | More than a slogan

Abelo repeatedly describes turboprops as part of the transition toward lower-emission regional aviation. ATR states that its aircraft emit **about 45% less CO₂ than similar-size regional jets**.

The useful interview point is not to repeat the ESG language mechanically. Erik should connect sustainability to the underlying economics:

**right-sized aircraft + lower fuel burn on suitable regional routes + access to smaller airports + replacement of older aircraft = a commercial as well as environmental proposition.**
